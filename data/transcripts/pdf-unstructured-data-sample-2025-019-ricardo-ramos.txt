The first prototype burned, because a design error, as it was brought to the physical limit (Phoenix 1)
— here I did revision one and two — There were many problems, when you are producing an aircraft
there is many regulations but because we were in the prototyping phase we skip a lot of norms and
regulations and standards. Imagine that you have the first connectors that you have in the battery
were very thin so didn’t stand resistant welding, and when you were welding them they failed as they
burned. The welding process was not a problem but all the things from the design perspective, like
electric and higher voltage systems were to the limit, also systems connecting to each other you
couldn’t see if the component was not connected in the socket. So lack of communication between
the design and manufacturing departments. We need to have better connection between design and
manufacturing.
